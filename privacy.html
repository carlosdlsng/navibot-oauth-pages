NaviBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Overview

NaviBot is a personal automation tool. This policy describes the intended handling of Google account information when an account owner explicitly authorizes NaviBot through Google OAuth.

Information accessed

Depending on the permissions the account owner grants, NaviBot may access Gmail message content, metadata, labels, and other email information needed to carry out requested email-management tasks. OAuth tokens are used to maintain authorized access.

How information is used

Google user data is used only to perform email workflows authorized by the account owner, such as retrieving, searching, processing, and organizing messages. NaviBot does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Authorization credentials and data needed for automation are handled within the operator's configured environment. Access to that environment should be restricted to authorized users. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

Sharing

NaviBot does not intentionally share Gmail content with advertisers or data brokers. Information may be processed by services explicitly configured by the operator to fulfill requested automation tasks. Such services should be assessed and configured by the operator before use.

Retention and deletion

Data and credentials are retained only as needed for configured workflows or until the operator removes them. To request removal of locally stored account data, contact the operator using the address below.

Revoking access

Users can revoke NaviBot's Google account access at any time through their Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future access but does not itself delete any data already stored locally.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact navibotmail@gmail.com.
