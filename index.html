NaviBot

NaviBot is a personal automation assistant used to organize and manage email workflows for accounts explicitly connected by their owner.

How it works

With the account owner's permission, NaviBot uses Google's OAuth authorization process to access Gmail features needed for authorized email workflows, such as searching, reading, and organizing messages. Access is limited by the permissions granted during Google sign-in.

NaviBot is not a public email service. It is intended for personal use by its operator and explicitly authorized accounts.
